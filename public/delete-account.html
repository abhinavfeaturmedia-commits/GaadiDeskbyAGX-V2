Account & Data Safety Compliance

Request Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store Policies and user privacy standards, you have the right to request permanent deletion of your GaadiDesk account and all associated operational data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the GaadiDesk mobile app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Menu (More) icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Settings section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Wipe Data.
  5. Confirm your decision. All business profiles, bookings, fleet records, customer contacts, and driver ledger entries will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Submit Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your phone, submit your registered mobile number below. Our support team will process your deletion request within 48 business hours.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Business Profile: Agency name, GSTIN, owner credentials, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Fleet Assets: Vehicle plates, insurance, fitness, PUC, permits, and inspection records.
  • Trips & Ledger: Active bookings, trip history, customer CRM contacts, driver bata, and GST invoices.
  • Cloud Backups: All synchronized database rows in Supabase and cached media files.

*Note: Financial tax records may be retained locally on your device if exported as CA Excel / PDF sheets for income tax compliance.
