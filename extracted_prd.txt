Product Requirements Document
GaadiDesk — Cab Booking & Car Rental Business App (India)
Version: 1.0Date: 30 August 2026Owner: Abhinav GaikwadStatus: Draft for build planningProduct type: B2B SaaS (membership)Primary platform (Phase 1): Android appSecondary platform (Phase 2): Web console for super-admin + business owners
1. One-line summary
GaadiDesk is a simple Android app where Indian cab and car-rental owners can register, pay a membership, and run bookings, cars, drivers, money, and papers from one place — without needing technical skill.
This is not another Uber for passengers.This is an office in the phone for the business owner.
Simple example:Ramesh in Kolhapur has 8 cars. Today he writes bookings in a diary, WhatsApps drivers, and forgets insurance expiry. In GaadiDesk he taps “New Booking”, picks car + driver, shares WhatsApp confirmation, and sees today’s cash vs pending.
2. Problem
Most small/mid cab and rental operators in India still run the business on:
Diary / Excel
WhatsApp chats
Memory
Paper RC, insurance, PUC
Manual GST bills
This creates daily pain:
Double booking the same car
No idea which car is free tomorrow
Driver took cash, owner does not know
Extra KM, driver bata, night charge, toll not added correctly
Insurance / PUC / fitness expired, car still sent on trip
No clean GST invoice for corporate clients
Owner cannot see full business from home
They will not use a complex “enterprise fleet ERP”. The app must feel as easy as WhatsApp.
3. Product vision
Phase 1: One Android app per business. Owner and 1–2 staff manage the whole operation.
Membership: Each car business pays monthly/yearly to use the app. Plans based on number of vehicles + staff.
Phase 2: A website where:
You (platform owner) see all businesses, payments, usage, support
Each business can also open a web dashboard on laptop
Later, optional public booking page per business
Long-term (not now): marketplace connecting travellers to member fleets. Do not build this in Phase 1.
4. Goals

Launch a paid membership product for Indian cab/rental operators

7. UX principles (most important section)
If UX is hard, the product fails. Non-technical owners will uninstall.
WhatsApp-simple. Home screen has 6 large tiles only.
Hindi + English from day 1. Later Marathi. Privacy text also in simple language.
Big tap targets. Minimum 48dp buttons. High contrast.
One job per screen. Do not put 20 fields on one page.
Smart defaults. Last used car, last rate card, today’s date already filled.
Voice + photo. Owner can speak customer name, or click RC photo to save vehicle.
WhatsApp share everywhere. Booking slip, invoice, driver details — share as WhatsApp message/PDF.
Works on cheap Android. Target Android 8+, 2GB RAM, slow 4G.
Offline-first for core actions. Create booking, mark payment, view today’s trips even if network drops. Sync later.
No jargon. Say “Car free?” not “Fleet utilization”. Say “Money not received” not “Accounts receivable”.

15. Compliance, trust, legal (India)
DPDP Act
App will store phone, address, DL, Aadhaar images, GSTIN, trip history.
Must-have from day 1:
Clear privacy notice in English + Hindi
Consent checkbox on signup
Purpose limitation: data only for running their cab business
Role-based access
Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest for documents
Ability to export / delete a business’s data
Breach process (notify Board without delay; industry practice often treats 72 hours as the working target)
No children’s data
Vendor contracts with OTP, SMS, storage providers
Data residency preference: India region
Full DPDP substantive duties phase in through 2026–May 2027. Design privacy now so you do not rebuild later.
Other
GST e-invoice / e-way is not MVP. Only GST-looking PDF + report for CA.
Do not store Aadhaar number as searchable text if you can avoid it; store image + last 4 digits if needed.
RBI/UPI: do not hold customer card data. Use payment gateway.
Play Store: privacy policy URL, data safety form, account deletion.
Terms: you are software vendor, not a transport company. Member business is responsible for permits, insurance, driver conduct.
